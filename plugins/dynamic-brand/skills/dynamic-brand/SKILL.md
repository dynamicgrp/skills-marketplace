# Dynamic Group Brand Standards

Source: Dynamic Group Brand Guide, September 2026. It governs proposals, reports and qualifications, and the voice rules govern every communication down to a two line email.

Apply these standards without being asked whenever the output is for Dynamic Group. Do not ask the user to confirm the palette or the fonts; they are settled here.

## Color

Two primary colors, both taken from the logo. Everything else is accent or neutral.

| Role | Name | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Dynamic Orange | `#F26F21` | Rules, ticks, bullets, card top borders, active bars. The cube. |
| Primary | Dynamic Charcoal | `#54565A` | Table header bands, small caps, chrome. The wordmark. |
| Accent | Deep Orange | `#D2570F` | Numerals, links, emphasis, labels |
| Accent | Amber | `#F5A623` | Cover gradients, alternate schedule bars |
| Ink | Char Dark | `#3C3D41` | Headings |
| Ink | Ink | `#2C2D31` | Body |
| Ink | Muted | `#6E7075` | Captions |
| Surface | Paper | `#FFFFFF` | Page |
| Surface | Panel | `#F5F4F1` | Panels |
| Surface | Panel 2 | `#EEEDE9` | Secondary panels |
| Surface | Viewport | `#E9E9E6` | Screen background behind the page |
| Line | Border | `#D8D9DB` | 1px borders |
| Line | Hairline | `#E7E8E9` | Interior table rules |

No blues. No purples. No gradients except the dark scrim over cover photography.

An earlier Dynamic palette used `#F58132` as the orange. It is superseded. Use `#F26F21`.

## Typography

Two families, and they do not overlap.

**Barlow Semi Condensed** sets every heading, label, numeral and piece of page chrome. ALL CAPS by preference, with wide tracking.

**Source Sans 3** sets body copy, tables and captions. Sentence case.

| Role | Size | Family | Casing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Section divider title | 40.5pt | Barlow Semi Condensed | ALL CAPS |
| Cover title | 33.75pt | Barlow Semi Condensed | ALL CAPS |
| Section heading | 22.5pt | Barlow Semi Condensed | ALL CAPS |
| Subsection heading | 13.5pt | Barlow Semi Condensed | ALL CAPS |
| Kicker, eyebrow | 9pt | Barlow Semi Condensed | ALL CAPS, .2em tracking |
| Lead paragraph | 12.4pt | Source Sans 3 | Sentence |
| Body, table cell | 11.25pt | Source Sans 3 | Sentence |
| Dense table, caption | 9.75pt | Source Sans 3 | Sentence |
| Footer chrome, legend | 8.25pt | Barlow Semi Condensed | ALL CAPS |

Body copy is 11.25pt. Never set an all caps run inside a paragraph. For emphasis inside body text use bold or Deep Orange.

## Voice

Dynamic speaks in the first person, plural. We are a company of people, not an institution describing itself from the outside.

- Use **Dynamic Group** in the opening of a document, then **Dynamic** thereafter.
- Refer to the company in the first person: "Dynamic is proud of our employees."
- Never refer to Dynamic as *itself*, *its*, or *the firm*.
- Address the client by title, not as *you*.
- Phone numbers are set `(###) ###-####`, with a space after the area code.

**Do:** active voice, concise and direct, emphasize outcomes and solutions, focus on client and community impact, plain language, let metrics carry the emphasis set large in Deep Orange.

**Avoid:** excessive jargon, overly promotional language, informal slang, unsubstantiated claims, aggressive or negative wording, repetition.

**Never use em dashes, emoji, or exclamation points.** This is a hard rule and applies to every Dynamic output including email and social. Rewrite the sentence rather than reaching for a dash.

### Tone by communication type

| Type | Tone |
|---|---|
| Proposals | Technical, precise, authoritative |
| Website | Informative, confident, approachable |
| Social media | Positive, community focused, engaging |
| Client emails | Professional, concise, collaborative |
| Crisis communications | Calm, reassuring, direct |
| Internal communications | Supportive, team oriented |

### Personality

Every Dynamic communication carries seven traits: Professional, Responsive, Resilient, Trusted, Technical, Human-Centered, Collaborative.

Dynamic should be perceived as a dependable partner, operationally capable, highly organized, technically sophisticated, mission driven, calm under pressure and community focused.

## Logo

Two assets. The full lockup carries the gray wordmark and the orange cube. The cube alone runs in page chrome where the wordmark would be too small to read.

- Always use the full color logo. Another version only when a client or contract requires it.
- One logo per page. If the mark sits in the running header it does not also appear in the footer.
- Clear space on all sides is at least the height of the cube.
- Minimum height 21pt, or 0.3in. Below that, use the cube alone.
- On paper the lockup runs at 30 to 42pt.
- The lockup sits top left on covers and dividers. The cube sits in the running header.
- Over photography the lockup sits on the standard cover scrim with a drop shadow. Never on a bare photograph, never in a box of its own.

Do not recolor either asset, including all white or all black versions. Do not stretch, rotate, outline or add effects beyond the permitted drop shadow. Do not place the lockup on a busy area of a photograph where the wordmark loses contrast. Do not rebuild the wordmark in Barlow or any other typeface.

## Layout and motifs

The recurring device is a short orange rule. The system is otherwise near square and icon free.

| Device | Spec |
|---|---|
| Kicker | 22px orange bar leading an uppercase eyebrow |
| Section rule | 5 to 6px orange beneath a divider title |
| Header underline | 2px orange under the running header |
| Card | 3px orange top border |
| Callout | 4px orange left rule |

Page is 816 by 1056px, US Letter at 96dpi, one page per printed sheet. Side padding 60px, and 56px on covers and dividers. House radius 2px; tables and matrices are square. Column gutter 22px.

Depth comes from solid fills, hairline borders and soft shadows. No glass, no blur. The only transparency is the scrim over cover photography.

## Lists

Four markers. The diamond is the house marker, but a document carried entirely by diamonds reads as one undifferentiated list. Pick one marker per page and let the others do work elsewhere.

| Marker | Class | Spec | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diamond | `ul.bul` | 6px orange square, rotated 45 degrees | Section level lists in narrative pages; scope, rules, requirements. The default. |
| Dash | `ul.bul--dash` | 9 by 2px orange dash | Dense capability lists inside a card; exclusions and do-not lists; long runs where the diamond becomes noisy |
| Dot | `ul.bul--dot` | 5px orange dot | Nested lists beneath a diamond parent; appendices, definitions, reference lists |
| Icon | `ul.bul--icon` | 15px single weight line, Deep Orange | Sequenced steps or a process, where the icon carries meaning |

Icons are single weight line only, 15px with a 1.6px stroke, set in `currentColor` so they inherit Deep Orange. Never filled, never duotone, never an icon font, never emoji. An icon list earns its place once or twice in a document.

## Imagery

Documentary photography of our sites, plant and crews. Never decorative, never stock styled.

- Covers and dividers take dark full bleed photography under a scrim so white type reads.
- Content pages take rectangular figures with the caption directly beneath.
- Captions name the client first, then what is shown, in uppercase.
- Photographs show facilities, sites, plant and crews at work.

## Applying this

When producing a document, build it to these tokens without asking. When reviewing existing material, check it against this order: voice prohibitions first (em dashes, emoji, exclamation points, third person self reference), then color and type, then layout and logo. Report what fails and what to change it to, not a general impression.
