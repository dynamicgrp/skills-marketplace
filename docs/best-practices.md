# Skill Writing Best Practices

## Do's ✅

- **Be specific to construction**: Reference actual Dynamic Group workflows
- **Provide real examples**: Use anonymized past projects
- **Set clear scope**: Explain what you DO and DON'T help with
- **Ask for data**: Incomplete information = incomplete analysis
- **Explain reasoning**: Help users understand your recommendations

## Don'ts ❌

- **Don't be too generic**: If 5 companies could use it unchanged, it's too broad
- **Don't skip examples**: Real usage scenarios are crucial
- **Don't assume expertise**: Explain technical terms
- **Don't claim expertise you don't have**: Stay in your lane
- **Don't ignore edge cases**: Limitations are important

## Structure

A good skill has:

1. **Opening**: 1-2 sentences on what you do
2. **Expertise section**: What domain knowledge you bring
3. **Input requirements**: What users need to provide
4. **Process section**: Step-by-step what you do
5. **Output format**: How you structure your response
6. **Guidelines**: Key principles and assumptions
7. **Questions**: What you ask if info is incomplete
8. **Boundaries**: When NOT to use this skill
